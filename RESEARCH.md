# Market Research for Navero

A structured breakdown of the EXIM landscape, major players, market gaps, and opportunities.

---

# 1. Total Addressable Market (TAM)

Global trade value is massive:

- **$32–34 trillion** total import/export value  
- **$23–25 trillion** handled by SMEs and mid-size companies  
- **80%+** of transactions involve customs, banking, freight, and broker interactions  
- **Digitization rate <20%**

Target layers: exporters, importers, brokers, banks, customs workflows.  
This segment touches **$6–8 trillion** worth of transactions annually.

---

# 2. Current State of Digitization

The EXIM workflow is still largely manual:

- Exporters: spreadsheets, emails, PDFs  
- Importers: similar issues  
- Brokers: outdated government portals  
- Banks: SWIFT + internal checks  
- Customs: country-specific systems with no standardization  

There is no unified workflow across stakeholders. Navero aims to unify these fractured processes.

There is **no platform** covering trade workflow + compliance + banks + customs for both exporters and importers. The gap is significant.

---

# 4. Core Problem Navero Solves

The primary bottleneck is **coordination, documentation, and compliance**, not shipping.

Key pain points:

- Document mismatches  
- Bank/LC discrepancies  
- Customs queries  
- Missing updates  
- Fragmented communication  
- Delayed payments  
- No single source of truth  
- Manual data re-entry  
- Poor visibility for stakeholders  

These factors cause shipments to extend from 20 days to 45 days. Navero streamlines the entire process.
