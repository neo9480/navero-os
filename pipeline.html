PHASE 02
Letter of Credit (LC) Issuance
Importer · HDFC Bank · JPMorgan
▼

Indian importer applies to HDFC Bank for a Letter of Credit guaranteeing payment to US exporter upon presentation of shipping documents.

What Happens

Nexus Imports applies to HDFC Bank Mumbai for an Irrevocable Documentary Letter of Credit (LC) for $999,500 USD in favor of TechTrade Inc. HDFC issues the LC and transmits it via SWIFT to JPMorgan Chase (USA) — the Advising/Confirming Bank. JPMorgan advises and confirms the LC to TechTrade Inc. This is the payment guarantee for the exporter. HDFC also charges a margin/collateral from Nexus Imports (typically 15–25% of LC value upfront, rest as working capital loan).

PHASE 05

Exporter · FedEx Freight · Insurer
▼

Cargo insurance is taken (CIF — exporter's responsibility). Container is booked on MSC container vessel from Oakland Port to Nhava Sheva (Mumbai).

Goods are packed in anti-static, custom palletized containers. Transit time: ~22–25 days.

Container is inspected or released by CBP at Oakland port. AES filing verified. Cargo loaded onto MSC vessel.

PHASE 07
Ocean Transit & Bill of Lading
MSC Shipping · Both Banks
▼

Container sails for 22–25 days. Final Bill of Lading issued. TechTrade presents shipping docs to JPMorgan to draw on the LC.

What Happens

MSC vessel departs Oakland and sails to Nhava Sheva, Mumbai (~22 days, route via Pacific). MSC issues the Original Bill of Lading (3 originals) to TechTrade Inc. TechTrade then assembles the full Document Package and presents it to JPMorgan Chase for negotiation under the LC. JPMorgan examines documents against LC terms (UCP 600 rules). On finding no discrepancies, JPMorgan pays TechTrade $999,500 and forwards the original docs to HDFC Bank in India for reimbursement.

PHASE 10
Indian Customs Import Clearance (CBIC)
CBIC · Customs Broker · Importer
▼

Bill of Entry filed on ICEGATE. BCD, IGST, and SWS computed and paid. MacBooks classified under HS Code 8471 and cleared.

What Happens

The customs broker files a Bill of Entry (BE) on the ICEGATE portal under HS Code 8471 30 20 (Laptops/Portable Computers). Assessable Value (AV) = CIF value converted to INR (at RBI rate: 1 USD = ₹83.50): ₹83,457,250. Customs duties assessed:

• Basic Customs Duty (BCD): 0% (laptops exempt under ITA)
• Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS): 10% of BCD = ₹0
• IGST: 18% of (AV + BCD + SWS) = ₹15,022,305

Total duty = ~₹15,022,305 (~$179,909 USD). Paid electronically via ICEGATE. Examiner examines/seals container. Out-of-Charge Order issued.

Documents Created

Bill of Entry (BE) HS Code Classification Sheet Duty Payment Challan (TR-6) Assessment Order Out-of-Charge Order Import License (IEC Copy) FSSAI / BIS NOC (electronics)

Duties & Fees

BCD: ₹0 (0% on laptops) IGST (18%): ₹15,022,305 (~$179,909) Customs Broker Fee: ₹85,000 (~$1,018) ICEGATE Filing Fee: ₹3,000 (~$36)

PHASE 14
Post-Shipment Compliance & Reporting
Both Parties · RBI · US Authorities
▼

FEMA reporting to RBI, US EEI reconciliation, GST input credit claimed by importer, and record-keeping obligations fulfilled.

What Happens (India Side)

TechTrade Inc. reconciles EEI filing with actual shipment. Files Form 1099 if applicable. Updates export records for EAR compliance. Retains all trade documents for 5 years (BIS requirement).
